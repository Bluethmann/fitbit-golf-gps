On phone (before google took over)
 On Your PhoneOpen the Fitbit App on your smartphone.
 Tap your Profile icon (top left corner) to open account settings.
 Select Developer Menu from the list of options.
 Toggle the Developer Bridge switch to ON.
 Ensure Bluetooth remains enabled and the app runs in the background.

On Your Sense 2 Watch
Place the watch onto its magnetic USB charging cradle connected to your computer.
Swipe down on the watch face and tap the Settings gear.
Scroll down and select Developer Options (or Developer Bridge).
Toggle USB Debugging to ON.
Keep the watch screen awake until the terminal connection establishes.
